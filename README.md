# Riverbank Erosion and Accretion Prediction Using Machine Learning

## Project Overview

Riverbank erosion and accretion are major environmental concerns in Bangladesh. Every year, changing river boundaries affect nearby communities, agricultural land, and natural ecosystems. Understanding these changes is important for better river management and environmental planning. This project explores the use of machine learning to analyze and predict riverbank erosion and accretion in five rivers of coastal Bangladesh: **Bishkhali, Baleshwar, Payra, Shibsa, and Pasur**. We use satellite imagery, remote sensing indices, and geographical information to study how riverbank areas change over time. The main goal is to identify patterns of erosion and accretion and explore how machine learning can support river monitoring and environmental research.

## Environmental Contribution

Riverbank erosion and accretion can have a lasting impact on both human lives and the natural environment. Through this project, we aim to better understand these changes using satellite data and machine learning. By identifying areas vulnerable to erosion, this research could help improve awareness of riverbank changes and their possible effects on nearby settlements, agricultural land, and vegetation. The analysis may also provide useful insights for monitoring river boundaries and understanding changes in surrounding land cover.
We hope this work can contribute to future environmental research and support data-driven approaches to river monitoring, riverbank protection, and sustainable land management in Bangladesh.
